# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want CineView discussions and contributions to be welcoming, technically
rigorous, and free from harassment. Participants should assume good faith,
give actionable feedback, respect different experience levels, and keep debate
focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes discrimination, harassment, threats, personal
attacks, sexualized language or attention, and publishing private information
without permission.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's
security or contact channel. Maintainers may clarify, remove, or reject
comments and contributions, and may temporarily or permanently exclude people
who violate this policy. Reports will be handled confidentially where possible.
