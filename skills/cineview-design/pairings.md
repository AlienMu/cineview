# Pairings

How the visual styles in `styles.md` go with motion, page modes, material, and each other.
Each line gives the reason, so a pairing listed as clashing can still be chosen on purpose,
knowing what to watch.

## Style and motion

- **Clean product + short rises and wipes.** The motion explains (this part, then that one)
  without decorating. Long drifts and loops pull attention from the interface.
- **Editorial paper + lines of text arriving one after another, rules drawn out.** It reads like
  turning to a new spread. Fast hard cuts break the unhurried tone.
- **Night neon + fast wipes, counts, footage scrubbed by the reader.** The energy matches the
  palette. Slow focus pulls feel sleepy against it.
- **Poster + hard wipes with linear easing, blocks covering blocks.** Flat colour wants edges
  that move like cut paper. Soft fades blur the graphic punch.
- **Soft lifestyle + focus, soft rises, a slight drift on photos.** Gentle motion keeps the warmth.
  Snappy, technical motion feels cold here.
- **Monochrome documentary + slow pushes, captions arriving after their photos, `duotone(mono)`.**
  The photo is seen first, and its date and place follow, as in an exhibition.
- **Playful + springy grows, loops, small rotations.** Overshoot reads as friendly. Keep it to a
  few elements at a time, or everything wobbles.
- **Luxury minimal + very slow pushes and long fades.** Slowness reads as care. Bounces and
  overshoot read as cheap.
- **Brutalist + abrupt switches, linear wipes, skew on fast scroll.** Motion that refuses to be
  smooth fits the voice.
- **Retro terminal + typing, stepped easing, a blinking cursor.** The motion of the machine it
  imitates.
- **Swiss grid + wipes along grid lines, lines entering in reading order.** Motion that shows the
  grid reinforces it.
- **Organic nature + lines drawing themselves, slow drift on layers.** Growth-like motion.
- **Cinematic + footage scrubbed by scroll or finger, slow pushes, cuts.** The page behaves like an
  edit.
- **East Asian paper + slow reveals, brush strokes drawn as paths, vertical text unrolling.** Ink
  appears as if written.
- **Handmade collage + pieces arriving one by one at slight angles.** Like pieces laid on a table.
- **Technical drawing + lines drawing in order, parts arriving in assembly order, labels
  following their parts.** The motion is the explanation.

## Style and page mode

- **Drag pages** carry one picture and a line per screen, so styles led by a single strong
  image work well there: cinematic, poster, night neon, monochrome documentary, soft lifestyle.
  Styles that live on long text, such as editorial paper with long reads or Swiss reports,
  need their text cut down to fit one screen.
- **Held scenes (zones)** suit styles that explain something step by step: clean product,
  technical drawing, Swiss grid.
- **Ordinary scroll** suits reading: editorial paper, documentary, organic nature.
- **Covers (stacked scenes)** suit poster and collage, where one piece laid over another is
  already part of the look.

## Style and material

- **Photographs**: documentary, lifestyle, luxury, organic nature. The photos set the palette.
- **Screenshots and interfaces**: clean product, Swiss grid, technical drawing. The interface's
  own colours set the accent.
- **Illustrations**: playful, collage, organic nature. The illustrator's palette leads.
- **Footage**: cinematic, night neon. The footage's grade leads.
- **Numbers and data**: Swiss grid, poster (numbers as graphics), technical drawing.
- **Calligraphy, seals, scans of paper**: East Asian paper, handmade collage.

## Mixing two styles

Mixes work when each side keeps one job. One supplies the type logic, the other the colour or
texture.
- **Editorial type + poster colour blocks**: a magazine-cover feel. Keep one family of fonts and
  use the blocks only at chapter openings.
- **Swiss grid + night neon**: a technical editorial look. The grid holds the layout and the glow
  is kept for numbers.
- **Documentary photos + clean product interface**: a product shown in real use. The photos stay
  monochrome and the interface carries the only colour.
- **East Asian paper + technical drawing**: craft explained precisely. Ink for titles, fine lines
  for diagrams.

## Pairings that tend to clash

- **Glowing text over photographs.** The glow and the photo's highlights compete, and the text
  loses its edge. Glow works on dark flat grounds.
- **Thin serifs small on dark grounds, especially on phones.** Hairlines vanish. Use larger sizes,
  a heavier cut, or a light ground.
- **Springy easing in luxury, documentary or East Asian paper.** Overshoot undoes the restraint.
- **Duotone over brand colours.** A two-ink print replaces the brand's colours inside the picture.
  Take the inks from the brand palette, or leave those pictures untouched.
- **Textures, grain or big shadows on scenes that move every frame** (drag pages, held scenes).
  They cost frames on phones (`phone.md`). Keep textures still, or on scenes that do not move.
- **Two display fonts at once.** They argue. Use one for display and a quiet one for the text.
